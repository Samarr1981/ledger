# Anchor index

Candidate material, by location. This is an index, not a question set. Go read each
section in the PDF, confirm it says what this claims, then decide whether it becomes
a question and what type it is. If a section doesn't survive your own reading, drop it.

Target mix: ~5 direct, ~3 multi-hop, ~3 numeric, ~3 trap, ~2 near-miss. 15 minimum.

## Multi-hop anchors (answer needs two separate sections)

- Coverage for "other automobiles" requires 2.2.3 (the coverage) plus the GVWR and
  regular-use conditions in the same subsection, plus the definition of *the automobile*
  in 1.3. Three hops if you write it carefully.
- Deductible interaction: 7.3 says the Section 7 deductible is the Collision deductible
  multiplied by your percentage at fault, but only makes sense alongside 6.4.2, which
  describes the DC-PD deductible being multiplied by the percentage NOT at fault.
  Different sections, opposite percentages. Strong multi-hop.
- Temporary substitute automobile: defined in 2.2.2, but the loss-or-damage treatment
  and the deductible-difference rule live in 7.4.3. The special condition (cannot be
  owned by anyone in your dwelling) is in 2.2.2 only.
- Cancellation notice periods differ by delivery method and by reason: 1.7.3 vs 1.7.4.

## Exclusion anchors (test whether chunking preserved parent coverage)

- 1.8.1 through 1.8.5 are general exclusions on the whole policy
- 7.2.1 general, 7.2.2 illegal use, 7.2.3 certain thefts, all specific to Section 7
- 3.5.1 through 3.5.3 specific to Liability
- The war exclusion in 1.8.5 excepts Liability Coverage. A chunk that loses that
  carve-out will generate a wrong answer.

## Near-miss anchors (OPCF 20 vs OPCF 27 vs base policy)

All three of these touch rental cars and none of them cover the same thing:

- OAP 1 s.7.4.4 - rental while YOUR car is stolen, $900 cap, base policy
- OPCF 20 s.2 - rental while YOUR car is being repaired, per-day and per-occurrence
  limits are blanks on the form. Explicitly REPLACES s.7.4.4, but s.2.3 says it will
  not pay less than 7.4.4's limits.
- OPCF 27 s.3 - damage to a car you DON'T own, e.g. a rental you're driving

Write at least one question that a careless system will answer by citing the wrong
one of these three. That's the near-miss category doing its job.

Also note OPCF 27 s.3.2.1 (GVWR 4,500 kg or less) versus OAP 1 s.2.2.4 special
condition 1, which allows over 4,500 kg for personal use if rented 7 days or fewer.
Adjacent, overlapping, different answers.

## Trap anchors (not in the corpus at all)

Confirm each of these really is absent before you use it:

- Any specific premium or dollar deductible (lives on the Certificate, not in the policy)
- Rental car excess or CDW charges in the United States
- Accident Benefits dollar amounts (s.4.1 defers entirely to the Statutory Accident
  Benefits Schedule, a separate document)
- Fault percentages for a specific accident scenario (s.6.4.1 defers to the Fault
  Determination Rules, also a separate document)

The last two are the best traps in the set, because the policy discusses the topic
at length while never stating the number. That is exactly the shape that produces a
confident, well-cited, wrong answer.
